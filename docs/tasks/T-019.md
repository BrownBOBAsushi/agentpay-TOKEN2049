# T-019 — Orchestrator: wait for the chain instead of stopping on a non-success settle reply
Milestone: M4 · Branch: feat/T-019-settle-poll · Status: todo · Depends on: T-017 merged (257ef11)

## Goal
Live rehearsal 04:13 UTC (test Mandate, Task `01a11491-3fab-76a9-b0b8-df7cce02ef17`): APPROVE, pay started
04:13:54, the seller answered after ~62 s with a reply that is neither success nor `settlement_pending`, and
`src/orchestrator/payment.ts:136` stopped with "payment did not settle". The tx
`37e05757f34867f5b55e5c2dd03fbfce1118247a3ebe3d8e005bf275c9bd550a` landed at 04:15:04 UTC (8 s later). A
re-run recovered it (`confirmed-on-chain`). The recorded S1 take must finish in **one** run.

## Approach (decided)
- Signed bytes either land before their TTL or never land. So after any non-success reply (other than a
  trusted success), do not throw at line 136: poll `deps.lookupTransaction(txHash)` every 5 s until found
  (→ `done`, `confirmed-on-chain`) or until the tx TTL (validTo, already parsed by `inspectHeaders`) plus 60 s
  grace has passed (→ stop, keep `prepared`, message "transaction expired unsettled; no payment").
  Never re-send to the seller after a final failure and never sign again.
- Log one line for the non-success reply with HTTP status and the x402 error/`errorReason` string only
  (no headers, no body, no secrets), e.g. `settle reply: 402 <reason>; waiting for chain`.
- Log line while waiting every ~15 s: `waiting for tx <hash> on preprod (<n> s)`.
