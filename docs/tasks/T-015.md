# T-015 — Teller's desk world + landing scroll sequence
Milestone: M3 · Branch: feat/T-015-desk · Status: todo
Depends on: T-014 merged.

## Goal
The user: "the idea is good, but how we present it looks boring, can't catch people's eyeballs" — make it
**realistic**. Stage the cheque world as a physical scene: cheques are paper objects on a teller's desk,
and the landing's refusal plays as one choreographed scroll moment. Binding: the updated OWN-WORLD and
FIRST VIEWPORT blocks in `.impeccable/surfaces/app-page-tsx.md`. References: Obys' Design Books
(Awwwards — tactile paper objects on a desk); bank practice: a bounced cheque returns as a
**Return Item** marked "Refer to Maker" and cannot be re-deposited (= the agent must go back to its human).

## Approach (decided — builder implements, does not re-decide)
**Desk** (`src/web/Desk.tsx`, global layout background for `/`, `/receipt`, `/mandate`):
- Base `#1d3b33`; leather grain as a repeating 256×256 SVG noise tile (`feTurbulence` fractalNoise
  ≈0.85, 2 octaves, rendered once to a data-URI CSS background, opacity ≈0.18) — never a full-page live
  filter. Lamp: radial highlight `rgba(255,236,200,.08)` from the top-left; vignette to `#13261f` at edges.
- Tooled gilt border: two thin lines `#b89b5e` (1px and 0.5px, 6px apart) inset 20px from the viewport
  edge on desktop (hidden < 640px).
- Text on the desk: headings `#e6ede7`, body `#c3d1c9` (state contrast ≥4.5:1 vs `#1d3b33`), links underlined.
- Selection, focus ring, scrollbar re-themed for the desk (focus ring `#e6ede7`, 2px, 3px offset).

**Paper objects** (extend `Cheque.tsx`, add `PaperObject.tsx`):
- Layered shadow: `0 1px 1px rgba(0,0,0,.28), 0 10px 20px rgba(0,0,0,.30), 0 28px 56px rgba(0,0,0,.28)`;
  a faint top-left sheen (linear gradient white 6% → 0). Tilt via prop (signed −1.2°, presented +2.5°).
- **Cheque-book stub**: left 17% of the cheque, separated by a vertical perforation (punched circles
  r=1.6px, pitch 7px, desk colour showing through). Stub shows: date (expiry), "To" (payee first 12…last 6),
  amount, nonce first 8. On mobile (< 640px) the stub becomes a top strip with a horizontal perforation.
- Keep every T-014 security-paper layer and the contrast rules.

**Landing** (`app/page.tsx`): first viewport per the contract. Then one pinned scroll scene (`position:
sticky` stage, ≈220vh tall section) that plays four steps by scroll progress (IntersectionObserver
sentinels or CSS `animation-timeline: view()` with IO fallback):
1. forged presented copy slides in from the right over the signed cheque (translateX 60%→8%, rotate 6°→2.5°);
2. red-pencil rings draw around the presented payee and amount (`#b3261e`, 2.5px, hand-drawn ellipse
   paths with slight overshoot, `stroke-dashoffset` draw 500ms, light `feTurbulence` roughness);
3. RETURNED stamp slams (reuse the existing press animation);
4. the **RETURN ITEM** slip slides down and is clipped to the forged cheque's top-right with an authored
   SVG paper clip (metal `#9aa3a7` with a highlight). Slip (≈300px, `#f3f4ef`, OCR-B): "RETURN ITEM ·
   AgentPay Guard · Guard Check / Reason: REFER TO MAKER / PAYEE_MISMATCH · AMOUNT_MISMATCH (computed by
   guardCheck) / This payment was not signed by its human. A new Mandate is required."
Without JS and under `prefers-reduced-motion`: render the final composed scene statically. No other
motion on the page. Below the scene: "the back of the cheque" endorsements on the desk (light text),
then the footer.

**Refuse** (unchanged): no kickers, no card grids, no gradient text, no glass, no new colours beyond the
contract, no emoji/Unicode icons (paper clip, rings, perforations are authored SVG).
